# Light professional marine-service website

Build a bright, calm and trustworthy multi-page website for yacht winter storage in Giżycko.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: responsive web, desktop and mobile
- Default theme: bright daylight; optional night mode
- Background: Warm White (#F7F8F5)
- Surface: Clean White (#FFFFFF)
- Primary Text: Marine Ink (#142C39)
- Structural Accent: Deep Marine (#153B4C)
- Action Accent: Signal Coral (#EE684D)
- Secondary Surface: Pale Sage (#EEF3F0)
- Typography: Manrope headings and DM Sans body copy
- Photography: natural sunny architectural boatyard photography, no dark overlay in daylight mode
- Cards: restrained 14–18 px corners, hairline borders, very soft shadows

**Page structure:**
1. Split-screen light hero with copy on white and a large bright boatyard photograph.
2. Compact four-item trust strip.
3. Three clear service cards: monitored yard, heated hall, winter service.
4. Yard facts section with confirmed parameters only. Do not show a top-down visualization until a real source image is provided.
5. Three seasonal price cards and a separate heated-hall row.
6. Coral reservation block and restrained marine footer.
7. Separate Offer, Pricing, Yard and Contact routes.
8. Mobile bottom navigation with icons and a highlighted contact action.

Never expose the technical PDF/PZT. Do not introduce unsupported certifications, testimonials, availability claims or 24/7 monitoring claims.
